M-Vave-OP1 ports parts of Emilie Gillet's eurorack code <https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack> (MIT):

- src/plaits_ws.c, the waveshaping model: Plaits, plaits/dsp/engine/waveshaping_engine.cc, the SLOPE
  shape of plaits/dsp/oscillator/oscillator.h, and from stmlib (stmlib/dsp/polyblep.h, dsp.h,
  parameter_interpolator.h) the integrated polyBLEP, the Hermite interpolation and the parameter ramps
- src/plaits_tables.h, tables of plaits/resources.cc (lut_sine, lut_fold, lut_fold_2, lut_ws_*)

Ported to C for the FM-1's FPU, 2026. The ported files stay under the MIT licence below; the rest
of Felucca is GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE). "Plaits" and "Mutable Instruments" name the source only; no
M-Vave-OP1 engine, model or preset is named after them.
